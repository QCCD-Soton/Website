We develop computational methods to understand molecular electronic structure, properties, and dynamics, combining classical quantum chemistry with emerging quantum-computing technologies. Our research spans quantum algorithm development, molecular spectroscopy, and quantum dynamics, aiming to make molecular simulations more accurate, efficient, and predictive.
