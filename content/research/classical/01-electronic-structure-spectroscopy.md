---
title: "Electronic structure theory and spectroscopy"
---

Accurate descriptions of molecular electronic states and properties are essential for understanding chemical processes and interpreting spectroscopic measurements. We develop and apply quantum-chemical methods to investigate ground and excited states, electronic correlation and molecular response to external fields.

Our research focuses on calculating spectroscopic observables, including X-ray absorption, photoelectron and scattering spectra. A current direction is **shake-down X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy (XPS)**, where we investigate how electronic relaxation accompanying core ionisation can provide insights into molecular electronic structure and excited-state dynamics.
