We make our algorithms accessible to the wider research community through the co-development of **SlowQuant**, an open-source software package for classical unitary wavefunction methods and quantum computational chemistry.

SlowQuant implements many of the methods developed in our research, including quantum linear-response theory and quantum error mitigation. It supports ideal quantum simulations, noisy quantum emulation and experiments on real quantum hardware, providing a platform for developing, testing and benchmarking quantum-chemistry algorithms.

**Software:** [SlowQuant on GitHub](https://github.com/erikkjellgren/SlowQuant)
