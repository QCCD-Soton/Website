We develop quantum algorithms for molecular electronic structure and properties, exploring approaches for both near-term and early fault-tolerant quantum computers. Our research combines algorithm development, error mitigation, hardware experiments and open-source software to advance accurate and resource-efficient quantum simulations of molecular systems.
