I am enrolled as PhD student at the Department of Chemistry at University of Copenhagen, with Prof. Stephan P. A. Sauer.
My PhD work is on quantum computing for quantum chemistry within the HQC2 collaboration, with a focus on open-shell molecules and the use of unrestricted wavefunction methods for molecular properties such as EPR hyperfine coupling constants.

I am a visiting PhD student at University of Southampton with the Ziems group from September to December 2026, working on unrestricted Quantum Krylov Subspace methods.
