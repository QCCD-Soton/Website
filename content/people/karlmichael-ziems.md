I’m fortunate to lead this brilliant team of researchers exploring quantum computing and quantum chemistry. My interests lie in understanding how we can harness quantum computers to simulate molecules and their properties. I develop electronic structure methods for classical and quantum computers and use them to unravel molecular quantum dynamics and understand how molecules interact with light.

Before joining Southampton, I worked as postdoctoral researcher at the Technical University of Denmark within the project “Hybrid Quantum Chemistry on Hybrid Quantum Computers” (HQC2). I have undertaken research stays at the Max Planck Institute for Chemical Energy Conversion, King Abdullah University of Science and Technology, and the University of Edinburgh.

Education:
- PhD in Theoretical Chemical Physics (Max Planck School of Photonics)
- MSc in Theoretical and Computational Chemistry (University of Oxford)
- BSc/MSc in Chemistry (Friedrich Schiller University Jena)
