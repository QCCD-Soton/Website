I work on the theory behind time-resolved X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy, developing simple rules that predict which extra “satellite” peaks show up in a spectrum as a molecule changes after absorbing light. I also model ultrafast photochemistry with computer simulations, and I’m currently preparing theory for an experiment to follow how 2-chlorothiophenol breaks apart.

Education:
- PhD Computational methods for the interpretation of ultrafast photochemical reactions, University of Edinburgh
